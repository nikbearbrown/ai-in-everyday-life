# Maple Row Community Bike Co-op: club knowledge

*This is an invented organization. Every detail below is made up for the workshop.*

**What we are.** A volunteer-run bike repair co-op. We teach people to fix their own bikes. We do not sell new bikes.

**Open hours.** Tuesday and Thursday, 5:00 pm to 8:00 pm. Saturday, 10:00 am to 2:00 pm. Closed Sunday, Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Closed on public holidays.

**Where.** The back room of the Maple Row community center, side entrance, next to the recycling bins.

**Membership.** Membership is $25 for a year. Members can use the tools and the repair stands during open hours. Non-members can come to a first visit for free.

**What to bring.** Your bike. A helmet if you plan to test ride. We have all common tools.

**Volunteer training.** New volunteers shadow an experienced volunteer for two open sessions. Interested people should come to any open session and ask for the volunteer lead.

**Donations.** We accept used bikes and parts that are in reasonable shape. We cannot take car parts or anything with a damaged frame. Drop-off is during open hours only.

**Repairs we do not do.** We do not repair e-bike batteries or motors. We do not do paid repairs. We are not a shop.

**Kids.** Anyone under 16 must come with an adult.

**Contact.** Emails are answered by volunteers, usually within a few days. We do not take phone calls.

**What we do not know.** Our knowledge does not cover refunds, insurance, injuries, lost items, special events, partnerships, or the schedule of other community center programs.
