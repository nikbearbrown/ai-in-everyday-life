# Before the day: get your free AI account

## Executive summary

**What this is.** One short task for every student, to finish a day or more before the workshop: make a free account with an AI assistant, and check it works. **Why it matters.** In the workshop you build something real with the AI, on your own account. If the account is not ready, you lose the first ten minutes. It takes about five minutes.

## What to do

1. **Make a free Claude account** at claude.ai. Use your own email. Accounts are for adults, so check the sign-up terms. Free accounts have usage limits that reset after a few hours, and the free plan's features can change, so look at what yours includes.
2. **Test it.** Send one message, for example: *"In two sentences, what does a spaceship's tail fin do in a cartoon?"* Check that it answers.
3. **Bring a laptop** with a modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox) and its charger. If you cannot bring one, tell the organizers ahead of time.
4. **Tell the organizers which build you would like**, in order of preference: an email assistant, a game, a 3D model, or a web app. You may not get your first choice.

## Rules for the AI on the day

- **Never put personal information** into the AI: no real names of other people, no student IDs, no addresses, no health information.
- **Paste only what you need.** Free accounts have limits. Short, specific messages go further.
- **Everything you build is invented.** No real people, no real emails.

## If you cannot make an account

Tell the organizers before the day. Your group can share an account, and you will still do the building.
