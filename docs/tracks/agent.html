An assistant that stops and asks

An email assistant for a small club you invent. You paste in an email. It summarizes it, drafts a short reply using only your club's own notes, and then stops so a person can approve it. Anything risky it flags for a human.

What to have open

The Claude app, in a new chat. Your club's notes in a short document.

The hour, in five moves

  1. Look. Read the sample club and the six test emails. Which ones should the assistant refuse to answer?
  2. Spec it. Write what the assistant must match, may decide, and must stop and ask about. Write each stop as a rule someone else could test. Then write your prediction: for each test email, draft or flag?
  3. Build. Paste your spec and your club's notes into Claude. Paste the emails one at a time.
  4. Catch. Compare what it did with your predictions. Write two emails of your own to break it. Find one real failure and fix the instructions, not just the answer.
  5. Ledger. Write down what the AI did, what you checked, who signs, and what you did not check.

Make it yours

Invent any organization: a community garden, a film society, a pet-sitting group, a board-game café. Decide what your assistant must never answer. Then write the emails most likely to trick it.

Bring this to the talk

The email that broke it, the rule you added, and one thing you would still not let it do.
