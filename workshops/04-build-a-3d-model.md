# 04. Build: A 3D Model (the Starship)

*Status: DRAFT, not yet dry-run. One group of about five, 65 minutes.*

## Executive summary

**What this is.** A group measures real engineering plans of a famous starship with a ruler, then has an AI build a 3D model in the browser that they can spin on screen, and compares it to the plans to find where the AI made things up. **Why this track.** It is the most physical track. It starts with a pencil and ends with an object. And it shows the central skill plainly: **you can see that the model is wrong because you measured the plan.** **What the group leaves with.** A rotatable 3D ship, a measurement sheet, and a list of every proportion the AI invented.

| **Roles** | Driver (keyboard), Measurer (ruler), Checker (compares to plan), Demo captain |

## The two jobs in this track

- **Master it:** Spec it. Turn a drawing into numbers, then the numbers into a prompt the AI can use.
- **Keep it human:** Compare the output to the world, and say what the model is not.

## Prep (coach, before the day)

Print the plans. Run the starter prompt once with your tool, and record how long it took and what it got wrong. Check that the 3D page opens in the browser, and decide how to run it offline (see the kit). Bring a finished example. **The plans are copyrighted franchise material and are not in the repository. Keep them classroom-only.**

## Run of show (65 minutes)

1. **Look (5 min).** Study the plan. List the parts: saucer, neck, hull, engine pods, pylons. Which are simple shapes? Which are hard?
2. **Measure (10 min).** Call the saucer diameter **1.0** on the top view. Measure the other parts against it. Write the numbers on the plan. Agree on them as a group.
3. **Spec it (5 min).** Three lists: **must match** (the proportions you measured), **may decide** (colors, glow), **must ask me** (anything not on the plan).
4. **Build (25 min).** The driver sends the plan photo and the numbers with the starter prompt. Open the result. Rotate it. Rotate the driver every eight minutes. Fix one proportion at a time.
5. **Catch (10 min).** Hold the screen next to the plan. Find where the model is wrong. Ask the AI to list every proportion it invented. Compare that list to what you found. Write the Catch Card.
6. **Ledger and demo prep (10 min).** Fill the ledger, including one line: **what this model is not** (a toy built from simple shapes, not a faithful model).

## The things to catch

A saucer that is the wrong thickness. Engine pods that are too close or too short. A "neck" that is a different width than the plan. A list of "invented proportions" that is shorter than the real list.

## Debrief questions

- Which of your measurements did the AI follow? Which did it ignore?
- If someone saw your model without the plan, what would they think the ship looks like?

## Unplugged fallback

If the AI chat or 3D page fails, build a cardboard-and-paper model from the measurements and describe the AI's job aloud. If the plan image is too small, measure a printed enlargement.

## Watch-outs

- Keep the plans and images of the model off public channels.
- A model that looks right from one angle may be wrong from another. Rotate it.
