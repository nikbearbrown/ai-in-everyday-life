# 09. The Driver's Canvas and the Ethics Cards

*Status: DRAFT, not yet dry-run. Shared by every lab.*

## Executive summary

**What this is.** The one-page worksheet every group fills in during a lab, plus a small deck of "what could go wrong" cards. This is also designed to be printed as a take-home card. **Why it exists.** It is the single place where the two commitments (master the tools, keep what is human) meet on paper. A group cannot finish without writing down what the AI did, how they checked it, and who signs. **What students leave with.** A one-page record of their own work that they can show an employer or a professor.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Time** | 10 minutes to introduce (inside the master class), then used all lab |
| **Print** | One canvas per group, plus one Ethics deck per table |

## The Driver's Canvas (print this)

**Group: ________  Track: ________  Date: ________**

**1. The problem, as observed.** Who is hurt, and how do we know? (*Observed, not generated.*)

**2. Two framings.** Framing A: ________ Framing B: ________ Would they need different fixes? Yes / No

**3. The spec.**
| Must match | May decide | Must stop and ask a human |
|---|---|---|

**4. What we built with AI.** (One line, plus the page or screen where it lives.)

**5. The Catch Card.** What the AI said: ________ What was true: ________ How we checked (with something that fails *differently* than the AI): ________

**6. The Driver's Ledger.**
| What the AI did | What we checked, and how | What a human decided and signs | Why that part stays human |
|---|---|---|---|

**7. What we did not check.** (One honest line. Required.)

**8. Our three-minute pitch:** problem, artifact, catch, ledger.

## The Ethics Cards (one deck per table)

Draw one when the group thinks it is done. It has to answer the card before pitching.

| Card | Question |
|---|---|
| **Whose voice is missing?** | Who would use this who is not in our group? What would they say? |
| **The wrong answer** | What happens to a real person if this is confidently wrong? |
| **The data** | Where does the information come from, and did anyone agree to it? |
| **The overruled human** | If a person disagrees with the AI, who wins, and is that written down? |
| **The hammock** | What skill will people lose if this always works? |
| **Access** | Who cannot use this at all? |
| **Who signs?** | Name the person who answers when it breaks. |

## Facilitator notes

- Collect a photo of each finished canvas **only if recording consent allows it**; otherwise let groups keep the paper.
- A ledger row that says "looks good" or "AI checked it" does not count. Ask: *looked at what, against what?*
- "What we did not check" is the most important line on the page. Praise honest ones loudly.
