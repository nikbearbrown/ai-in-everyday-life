# Run of show

## Executive summary

**What this is.** How the workshop fits together: the day in minutes, who runs what, how groups form and what each track needs. **Why read it.** Session length varies, so the day is built from modules that fit 90 minutes, 2 hours or a half day. **What it decides.** One short intro, then four groups each **build one real thing** (an agent, a game, a 3D model, a web app) in about an hour, then demos. Every group does two things at once: master the tools, and keep what is irreducibly human. See [THE-THROUGH-LINE.md](THE-THROUGH-LINE.md).

## The day (120 minutes, default)

| Clock | Module | What students do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | [01 Intro](workshops/01-intro-the-world-has-changed.md) (20 min) | A student builds live, the room catches the AI being wrong, coaches show finished work |
| 0:20 | Break and pick a track (5 min) | Students choose a track, five per group |
| 0:25 | The build (65 min) | Each group builds one thing, below |
| 1:30 | [06 Demos](workshops/06-the-ledger-and-the-demos.md) (20 min) | Each group runs its build live and answers for it |
| 1:50 | [07 Close](workshops/07-close-your-next-step.md) (10 min) | Graduate students and the anchor |

## The four builds (about an hour each)

| Track | Guide | They make | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent | [02](workshops/02-build-an-agent.md) | An email assistant that drafts replies and stops for a person's approval | A chat AI with saved instructions and a knowledge file |
| Game | [03](workshops/03-build-a-game.md) | A changed Claude Jumpman platformer in Godot | Godot 4.7.2 on the coach's laptop, an AI chat |
| 3D model | [04](workshops/04-build-a-3d-model.md) | A rotatable cartoon delivery ship built from reference photos | Printed photos, a ruler, an AI chat that accepts photos |
| Web app | [05](workshops/05-build-a-web-app.md) | A reskinned Oregon Trail game with a new feature | A browser and an AI chat |

Starter files for each are in [kits/](kits/).

## Every build has the same five steps

1. **Look** (5 min): play it, read it or measure it.
2. **Spec it** (7 min): write what the AI must match, may decide, and must stop and ask about.
3. **Build** (28 min): a student drives the keyboard, the coach does not, and the driver rotates every eight minutes.
4. **Catch** (10 min): find one thing the AI got wrong, using a check that fails differently than the AI.
5. **Ledger and demo prep** (10 min), plus 5 minutes of buffer.

## Other lengths

| Block | 90 min | 120 min | 180 min |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | 15 | 20 | 20 |
| Break and pick | 5 | 5 | 10 |
| The build | 45 (cut the buffer, skip the second driver rotation) | 65 | 90 (add a second change) |
| Demos | 16 | 20 | 30, with recognitions |
| Close | 9 | 10 | 25, with graduate student Q&A |

## Roles (about 6 graduate students)

| Role | Count | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Intro lead and emcee** | 1 | Runs 01, keeps the clock, builds the Error Bank, runs the demos |
| **Track coaches** | 4 | One per track. Owns a guide, coaches one group the whole hour, helps them demo |
| **Documenter** | 1 | Photos and recording for films, **only with confirmed consent** |
| **Faculty or staff anchor** | 1 | Runs the close, answers admissions questions |

Recruit 7 or 8 to land 5 or 6. One coach, one group, the whole build.

## Group formation

20 students, four groups of five, student choice, balanced by experience. Every group gets at least one student who has written code and at least one who has not. Say it aloud: *the coder is not the leader; the person who understands the user is.*

## Equipment (decision pending)

| Track | Needs |
|---|---|
| All | One laptop per track with an AI assistant, a projector, working Wi-Fi |
| Game | **Godot 4.7.2 installed** on that laptop (free, no installer, no account) |
| 3D | A printer for the photos, rulers, pencils |
| Agent | An AI tool with saved instructions and a knowledge file; test the instruction length cap |

Each coach tests their track on the real laptop with the real AI on a different day. Without that, no one knows if it fits in an hour.
